I’m a PhD researcher at McGill University working on machine learning for reliable visual systems.

My current work spans vision-language learning, anomaly detection, and the structure of pretrained representations. I’m especially drawn to the mathematical structure of high-dimensional representation spaces, and to how their counterintuitive geometry shapes sampling, directions, and model behavior.

I’m currently interested in research internships for 2027.
